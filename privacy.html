Privacy Policy — Sakuga Enhancer

Last updated: September 2026

Sakuga Enhancer is a client app for browsing sakugabooru.com. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

What the app does with your data

  • Search and browsing. Tag searches, show lookups, and post views are sent directly to sakugabooru.com's public API to fetch results. No separate server operated by this app's developer is involved.
  • Login credentials. If you log in, your username and a hashed version of your password (never the plain password) are stored only in your device's secure system keychain (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), never transmitted anywhere except to sakugabooru.com itself when logging in or posting a comment.
  • Comments you post. Sent directly to sakugabooru.com under your account, same as posting through their website.
  • Local caching. A small tag dictionary is cached on your device only, purely to make search faster. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Video trimming and downloads. Clips you trim or download are processed and saved entirely on your device.

What this app does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No data sold or shared with third parties
  • No account or data collected by the app's developer — sakugabooru.com is a separate, independent site this app connects to on your behalf

Third-party service

All content, accounts, and comments belong to and are governed by sakugabooru.com, an independent site not affiliated with this app's developer. Their own privacy practices apply to any account you create there.
